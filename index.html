About Us

Shaping Tomorrow's
Leaders Today

Ubulinga Secondary School has been a pillar of education in Bergville, KwaZulu-Natal since 1999. With over 1,000 learners and dedicated educators, we are committed to academic excellence, character development, and community upliftment.

🎯

Academic Excellence

We set high standards and support every learner to reach their full potential across all subjects.

🤝

Community Values

Rooted in Ubuntu — we grow together, support one another, and give back to our community.

🌟

Holistic Development

Beyond textbooks — sports, arts, and leadership programs shape well-rounded individuals.

Sports & Activities

Active Body,
Active Mind

Sport builds discipline, teamwork, and leadership. At Ubulinga we take pride in our sporting culture and encourage every learner to participate.

⚽

Football

Our football teams compete at district and provincial level. We develop technically skilled and tactically aware players who represent the school with pride.

🏐

Netball

Our netball program builds strong, confident young women. Teams train regularly and compete in KZN league competitions throughout the year.

🏃

Athletics

Annual athletics days and inter-school competitions keep our learners active and healthy, building speed, strength and stamina.
